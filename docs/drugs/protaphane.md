!!! note

---

## Preparation

!!! info "Before you start"
    Check the expiry date and vial integrity before opening. Label every syringe immediately after drawing up — never leave unlabelled syringes on the tray. **Resuspend** the insulin by gently rolling the vial between your palms until the suspension is uniformly cloudy.

### Equipment needed
- 1 × Protaphane 100 U/mL (10 mL vial)
- Normal saline (NS) 0.9% — for IDT dilutions
- 1 mL syringes × 3 (for test doses/dilutions)
- 25 G needles

### Step 1 — Prepare the SPT solution (100 U/mL — neat)
1. Gently resuspend the vial by rolling between palms.
2. Draw up **0.5–1 mL** of the stock (100 U/mL) into a 1 mL syringe.
3. Label: **"Protaphane SPT 100 U/mL"**.

### Step 2 — Prepare IDT dilutions
Prepare in this order, using a fresh syringe for each step.

1. **IDT 5 U/mL (1:20):**
    - Draw **0.1 mL** of stock (100 U/mL) into a 1 mL syringe. Add **1.9 mL NS**. Mix. (This is **5 U/mL — 1:20**).
    - Label: **"Protaphane IDT 5 U/mL"**.
