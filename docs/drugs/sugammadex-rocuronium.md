# Sugammadex (+ Rocuronium)
<!-- scratch:review-banner -->

## Overview
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Drug class | Neuromuscular reversal agent, tested as a 1:1 mix with rocuronium |
| Presentation | Sugammadex 100 mg/mL mixed with Rocuronium 10 mg/mL, equal volumes |

---

## Preparation
Mix sugammadex and rocuronium 1:1 immediately before use. The neat mixture is 100 mg/10 mg/mL. Prepare serial dilutions with normal saline.

---

## Skin prick test (SPT)
<!-- scratch:spt:rocuronium -->

**Interpretation:** Wheal ≥3 mm vs negative control = positive.

---

## Intradermal test (IDT)

### Dilution series
<!-- scratch:idt:rocuronium -->

**Inject:** 0.05 mL intradermal. Read at 15–20 minutes.
