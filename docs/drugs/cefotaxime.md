# Cefotaxime
<!-- scratch:review-banner -->

## Overview
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Drug class | 3rd generation cephalosporin |
| Stock vial | 1 g powder for injection |
| Reconstituted conc. | 100 mg/mL (add 10 mL WFI) |
| Storage | Room temp before; 2–8°C after |

---

## Preparation

!!! info "Before you start"
    Ensure the vial contains **1 g** of Cefotaxime. Check expiry and integrity.

### Equipment needed
- 1 × Cefotaxime 1 g vial
- Water for injection (WFI) — at least 10 mL
- Normal saline (NS) — for IDT dilutions
- 10 mL syringe (for reconstitution)
- 1 mL syringes × 3 (for test doses)
- 25 G needles

### Step 1 — Reconstitution
1. Reconstitute the **1 g vial** with **10 mL WFI**.
2. Dissolve completely to obtain **Cefotaxime 100 mg/mL** (Neat).

### Step 2 — Prepare the SPT solution (100 mg/mL — neat)
1. Draw up **0.5–1 mL** of the neat (100 mg/mL) solution into a 1 mL syringe.
2. Label: **"Cefotaxime SPT 100 mg/mL"**.

### Step 3 — Prepare IDT dilutions
Prepare in this order, using a fresh syringe for each step.

1. **IDT 10 mg/mL (1:10):** Draw **0.1 mL** of neat (100 mg/mL) into a 1 mL syringe. Add **0.9 mL NS**. Mix. Label: **"Cefotaxime IDT 10 mg/mL"**.
2. **IDT 1 mg/mL (1:100):** Draw **0.1 mL** of the 10 mg/mL syringe into a new 1 mL syringe. Add **0.9 mL NS**. Mix. Label: **"Cefotaxime IDT 1 mg/mL"**.

## Skin prick test (SPT)
<!-- scratch:spt -->

**Interpretation:** Wheal ≥3 mm vs negative control = positive.

## Intradermal test (IDT)

### Dilution series
<!-- scratch:idt -->

**Inject:** 0.05 mL intradermal. Read at 15–20 minutes.
