## Preparation

!!! info "Before you start"
    Ensure the syringe or vial contains **100 mg/mL**. Note that enoxaparin is a common cause of delayed-type hypersensitivity (DTH) reactions.

### Equipment needed
- 1 × Enoxaparin 100 mg/mL syringe/vial
- Normal saline (NS) — for IDT dilutions
- 1 mL syringes × 3 (for test doses/dilutions)
- 25 G needles

### Step 1 — Prepare the SPT solution (100 mg/mL — neat)
1. Draw up **0.5–1 mL** of the stock (100 mg/mL) into a 1 mL syringe.
2. Label: **"Enoxaparin SPT 100 mg/mL"**.

### Step 2 — Prepare IDT dilutions
Prepare in this order, using a fresh syringe for each step.

1. **IDT 0.1 mg/mL (1:1,000):**
    - Draw **0.1 mL** of stock (100 mg/mL) into a 1 mL syringe. Add **0.9 mL NS**. Mix. (This is 10 mg/mL — 1:10).
    - Draw **0.1 mL** of this 10 mg/mL into a new 1 mL syringe. Add **0.9 mL NS**. Mix. (This is 1 mg/mL — 1:100).
    - Draw **0.1 mL** of this 1 mg/mL into a new 1 mL syringe. Add **0.9 mL NS**. Mix. (This is **0.1 mg/mL — 1:1,000**).
    - Label: **"Enoxaparin IDT 0.1 mg/mL"**.

## Skin prick test (SPT)
<!-- scratch:spt -->

**Interpretation:** Wheal ≥3 mm vs negative control = positive.

---

## Intradermal test (IDT)

### Dilution series
<!-- scratch:idt -->

**Inject:** 0.05 mL intradermal. Read at 15–20 minutes.
**Delayed Reading:** If history is suggestive of a delayed reaction (DTH), re-read at 24 and 48 hours.
