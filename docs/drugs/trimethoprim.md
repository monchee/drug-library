# Trimethoprim
<!-- scratch:review-banner -->

## Overview
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Drug class | Dihydrofolate reductase inhibitor |
| Formulation | Oral suspension (trimethoprim-only) |

---

## Preparation
Use a calibrated oral syringe; shake well before each draw.

---

## Oral graded challenge
<!-- scratch:challenge:oral-graded-challenge -->

**Interval:** Observe for 20 minutes between steps; monitor for 1–2 hours after the final dose. For combination trimethoprim/sulfamethoxazole testing see Bactrim.
