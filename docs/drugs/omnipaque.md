# Omnipaque (Iohexol)
<!-- scratch:review-banner -->

## Overview
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Drug class | Low-osmolar non-ionic iodine-based contrast medium |
| Stock formulation | 350 mg I/mL (Common) or 300 mg I/mL |
| Reconstituted conc. | N/A (Ready to use) |
| Storage | Room temp; Protect from light and X-rays |

---

## Preparation

!!! info "Before you start"
    Check the iodine concentration. Usually **350 mg I/mL**. Ensure the solution is at room temperature and clear.

### Equipment needed
- 1 × Omnipaque 350 mg I/mL vial/bottle
- Normal saline (NS) — for IDT dilutions
- 1 mL syringes × 3 (for test doses/dilutions)
- 25 G needles

### Step 1 — Prepare the SPT solution (350 mg I/mL — neat)
1. Draw up **0.5–1 mL** of the stock (350 mg I/mL) into a 1 mL syringe.
2. Label: **"Omnipaque SPT 350 mg I/mL"**.

### Step 2 — Prepare IDT dilutions
Prepare in this order, using a fresh syringe for each step.

1. **IDT 1:10 (35 mg I/mL):**
    - Draw **0.1 mL** of stock (350 mg I/mL) into a 1 mL syringe. Add **0.9 mL NS**. Mix. (This is **35 mg I/mL — 1:10**).
    - Label: **"Omnipaque IDT 1:10"**.

2. **IDT 1:100 (3.5 mg I/mL):**
    - Draw **0.1 mL** of the 1:10 syringe into a new 1 mL syringe. Add **0.9 mL NS**. Mix. (This is **3.5 mg I/mL — 1:100**).
    - Label: **"Omnipaque IDT 1:100"**.

## Skin prick test (SPT)
<!-- scratch:spt -->

**Interpretation:** Wheal ≥3 mm vs negative control = positive.

---

## Intradermal test (IDT)

### Dilution series
<!-- scratch:idt -->

**Inject:** 0.05 mL intradermal. Read at 15–20 minutes.
**Delayed Reading:** If history is suggestive of a delayed reaction, re-read at 24 and 48 hours.
